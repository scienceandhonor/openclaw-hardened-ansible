# Stoic Reference Card

Use this as a seed for coaching, not as a script. Do not lecture from it.

## The Four Virtues

| Virtue | Core idea |
|--------|-----------|
| **Wisdom** (phronesis) | Knowing what is good, bad, and indifferent; correct judgement |
| **Courage** (andreia) | Acting rightly in the face of difficulty, pain, or fear |
| **Justice** (dikaiosyne) | Treating others as they deserve; serving the common good |
| **Temperance** (sophrosyne) | Self-regulation; not being mastered by appetite or emotion |

## Key practices

**Dichotomy of control** — Some things are up to us (judgements, desires, actions);
everything else is not. Suffering comes from treating externals as goods or evils.
Coaching question: *"Which column does that actually belong in?"*

**Negative visualisation** (premeditatio malorum) — Imagine losing what you value.
Sharpens appreciation; reduces shock. Not pessimism — preparation.
Coaching question: *"What would it look like if this didn't work out? And then what?"*

**View from above** — Zoom out: city, country, planet, cosmos. Your particular
irritation is one pixel in an enormous canvas. Useful for proportionality.
Coaching question: *"How does this look from 100 years out?"*

**Memento mori** — Remember you will die. Not morbidity; urgency. Are you spending
your day on what you'd choose on your last?

**Evening review** — Three questions before sleep: What did I do well? What could
I have done better? What have I learned?

**Morning preparation** — Anticipate the day's obstacles. Who will I encounter? Where
might I be tested? What role do I want virtue to play?

**Amor fati** — Love of fate. Not resignation; affirmation. What is, is. Work with it.

## Source authors

- **Marcus Aurelius** — *Meditations*. Roman emperor, practical. Most grounded.
- **Epictetus** — *Discourses*, *Enchiridion*. Former slave. Sharpest on control/freedom.
- **Seneca** — *Letters to Lucilius*, *On the Shortness of Life*. Most literary. Best on time.
- **Musonius Rufus** — Less known, very direct on practice and daily life.
